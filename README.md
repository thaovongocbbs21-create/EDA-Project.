Business Analytics Portfolio – MIS311 Assignment

# Project Overview

This project presents an Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) of a dataset containing information about Average Monthly Income, Cost of Living, Region, and Year.

The objective of this project is to explore the dataset, clean the data, generate descriptive statistics, and visualize important patterns. The analysis aims to better understand income distribution, regional differences, and the relationship between average monthly income and cost of living.

---

# Dataset Information

**Dataset:** Average Monthly Income and Cost of Living

### Dataset Summary

- Number of observations: **200**
- Number of variables: **4**
- Missing values identified: **5**
- Duplicate records: **0**

### Variables

- Year
- Average Monthly Income (USD)
- Cost of Living (USD)
- Region

---

# Data Cleaning

Before conducting the analysis, several preprocessing steps were performed to improve data quality.

The following tasks were completed:

- Imported the dataset into Microsoft Excel.
- Checked the dataset structure and variable types.
- Identified 5 missing values.
- Filled the missing values using appropriate methods.
- Checked for duplicate records.
- Verified that all numerical variables were stored correctly.
- Prepared the cleaned dataset for visualization and analysis.

After data cleaning, the dataset contained no duplicate records and was ready for exploratory analysis.

# Data Visualizations

## 1. Relationship between Average Monthly Income and Cost of Living

![Scatter Plot](images/scatter_plot.png)

### Interpretation

The scatter plot illustrates the relationship between Average Monthly Income and Cost of Living.

The points are widely dispersed across the graph, indicating that there is **no strong linear relationship** between the two variables. Individuals with both low and high income levels may experience different living costs.

---

## 2. Distribution of Average Monthly Income

![Histogram](images/histogram_income.png)

### Interpretation

The histogram shows the distribution of Average Monthly Income.

The income values are relatively spread across different income intervals, suggesting a balanced distribution without significant skewness.

---

## 3. Average Monthly Income by Region

![Bar Chart](images/bar_region.png)

### Interpretation

The bar chart compares the average monthly income among six different regions.

According to the analysis:

- Africa has the highest average monthly income.
- Oceania and Europe also report relatively high average incomes.
- North America has the lowest average monthly income.
- Asia and South America fall near the overall average.

This visualization highlights regional differences in average income.

---

# Key Insights

### Insight 1

The scatter plot indicates that there is **no strong correlation** between Average Monthly Income and Cost of Living. Higher income does not always correspond to higher living costs.

### Insight 2

Regional differences are clearly observed. Africa records the highest average monthly income, while North America has the lowest average income among the six regions included in the dataset.
